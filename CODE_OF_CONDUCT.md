# Code of conduct

PrepMate contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of background, identity, experience,
or ability.

Be constructive, focus criticism on the work, respect privacy, and do not post
another person's private information. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or retaliation are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the public support route listed on
the official PrepMate website or through the private repository maintainer.
Do not escalate them in public threads or include private user data.
Maintainers may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
exposing reporters or affected people.
